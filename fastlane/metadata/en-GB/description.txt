Arcfin is a premium, ground-up native client for Jellyfin — the free, open-source media server you run yourself. No subscriptions, no ads, no account with us. Point Arcfin at your own server and get a fast, polished, truly native experience — not a wrapped web view.

REQUIRES A JELLYFIN SERVER
Arcfin is a client, not a media library on its own. You'll need access to a Jellyfin server (self-hosted or one you've been invited to) to use it. Don't have one yet? Jellyfin is free and open-source — see jellyfin.org.

BUILT FOR iOS 26
A true SwiftUI app designed around iOS 26 and iPadOS 26's Liquid Glass — not a wrapped web view, not a cross-platform port. Fast, native navigation throughout, with a proper iPad layout.

PLAYBACK, DONE RIGHT
• Direct play and transcoded streaming, with automatic quality stepping if your connection can't keep up
• A second playback engine (VLC) for containers and codecs AVFoundation can't open
• AirPlay and Google Cast, with full Now Playing / Control Center integration
• Picture in Picture
• Judder-free playback with optional display frame-rate matching
• Custom subtitle styling — size, colour, and background
• Chapters, skip intro/credits/recap, playback speed control, and an Up Next card with an "are you still watching" check for long binge sessions

DOWNLOAD FOR OFFLINE
Download movies and episodes for offline playback, with Wi-Fi-only and quality controls, a Live Activity for progress, and smart storage management.

DISCOVER & TRACK
• Home screen with Continue Watching, Next Up, and Recommended sections
• Full library browsing with sorting (including by genre), filtering, and search
• Cast and crew pages
• Optional trailer playback, with an in-app YouTube fallback when a title has no local trailer
• Connect Seerr to browse and request new content without leaving Arcfin
• An optional Stats tab with your watch time, habits, streaks, top shows, genres and favourite actors, all from your own server, plus a card to share

BUILT-IN PRIVACY
• A profile picker with optional per-profile Face ID or PIN lock
• SyncPlay to watch together with others on your server, in sync
• No analytics, no tracking, no third-party ad SDKs — Arcfin talks directly to your own server and nowhere else

Arcfin is actively developed and updated. Feedback and bug reports are always welcome — see the Support link on the app's product page.
